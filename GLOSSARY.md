# Cadence

An online course platform where instructors publish courses and learners buy and take them.

## Language

### Purchasing

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a whole Purchase, which an Admin either approves or declines, and which is final once decided. A Purchase has at most one Refund, and a declined Refund returns no money.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback
